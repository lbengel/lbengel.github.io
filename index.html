02

Research

My work lies at the intersection of nonlinear partial differential equations, dynamical systems, and nonlinear optics. This includes interdisciplinary collaborations with DESY’s Ultra-fast Nonlinear Microphotonics Group (Hamburg), the Nonlinear Photonics ERC Lab (Rome), and KIT’s Optical Communications Group.
