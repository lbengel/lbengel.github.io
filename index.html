Lukas Bengel

I am an applied mathematician working in the group of Prof. Jens Rademacher at the University of Hamburg, where I have been a postdoctoral researcher since October 2026. From October 2025 to September 2026, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the Collaborative Research Center “Wave Phenomena” at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). In October 2025, I obtained my PhD in mathematics at KIT under the supervision of Prof. Wolfgang Reichel.

Research

My research lies at the intersection of nonlinear partial differential equations, dynamical systems, and nonlinear optics. I study the existence and dynamics of nonlinear waves in dissipative and Hamiltonian systems, with a particular interest in applying mathematical techniques to problems arising in microphotonics. This includes interdisciplinary collaborations with DESY’s Ultra-fast Nonlinear Microphotonics Group (Hamburg), the Nonlinear Photonics ERC Lab (Rome), and KIT’s Optical Communications Group.
